Project overview

The project

Konnect Services were appointed to deliver the mechanical and electrical installation package for the NW Works commercial fit-out, providing modern building services for high-quality office accommodation.

Working closely with the client, design team and principal contractor, we delivered coordinated M&E installations that supported the transformation of the workspace into a modern, efficient and compliant commercial environment.

Our team maintained high standards of workmanship throughout the project, ensuring all services were installed safely, efficiently and in accordance with the construction programme while meeting current building regulations and client expectations.
