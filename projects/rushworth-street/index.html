Project overview

The project

Konnect Services were appointed to deliver the mechanical and electrical services for the transformation of an existing industrial warehouse at 33–38 Rushworth Street into modern, spacious office accommodation.

Working closely with the client, design team and principal contractor, our team delivered coordinated building services to support the redevelopment, ensuring the new office environment was practical, efficient and aligned with the requirements of the completed space.

The project transformed a previously underutilised industrial building into a contemporary working environment, combining spacious office accommodation with carefully integrated mechanical and electrical services. Throughout the project, Konnect Services maintained a high standard of workmanship, delivering the installation safely and efficiently, with careful attention to detail and coordination across the wider construction programme.
