Project overview

The project

Konnect Services were appointed to design the electrical layout and deliver the mechanical and electrical services for the Clarendon Road development, a prestigious mixed-use residential scheme in North London.

Working alongside the principal contractor and wider project team, our engineers delivered high-quality M&E installations that met strict programme requirements, safety standards and technical specifications.

This landmark development combines modern residential accommodation with commercial space, creating a sustainable environment designed for long-term community living. Our experienced team ensured every stage of the mechanical and electrical installation was completed efficiently, maintaining exceptional quality while supporting the overall construction programme.
