Project overview

The project

Konnect Services was appointed to design and install the solar photovoltaic (PV) system for the refurbishment of an industrial warehouse and office facility at Abbey Road Industrial Estate.

Our team delivered a tailored renewable energy solution designed to improve energy efficiency, reduce operating costs and support the building’s sustainability goals.

Working closely with the client and project team, we ensured the solar PV system was seamlessly integrated into the development, delivering a high-quality installation completed safely, efficiently and in full compliance with current industry standards.
