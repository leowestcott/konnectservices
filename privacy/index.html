Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how Konnect Services Limited (“Konnect Services”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses personal information through this website.

Who we are

Konnect Services Limited, 49 Darkes Lane, Potters Bar, Hertfordshire, EN6 1BJ. Email: contact@konnectservices.com. Telephone: 01707 651550.

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry using our contact form we collect the details you provide: your name, company name, email address, telephone number, the type of enquiry, the project location and your message. If you contact us by email or telephone we will hold the information you give us.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare quotations and carry out any work you ask us to do.
  • To keep appropriate business records.

Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and, where you ask us for a quotation or services, taking steps to enter into a contract with you. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Form processing

Enquiry form submissions are processed by Formspree, Inc., a third-party form service, which delivers your message to us by email. Formspree may process data outside the UK; see Formspree’s privacy policy for details of its safeguards.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Links to Google Maps and Instagram take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information only for as long as is necessary to respond to you, provide any services you request and meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@konnectservices.com.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
