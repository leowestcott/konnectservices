01 — About Konnect

Since 2007, Konnect Services has delivered mechanical and electrical contracting for commercial, residential and industrial environments.

We are accredited installers of renewable energy systems. With a firm commitment to excellent customer service, quality products and clean energy, we offer a comprehensive design-to-installation service.

As a company, Konnect Services offers an in-house team — from design and technical, to our fully qualified engineers and testers. All our installations are fully managed and monitored internally, so we deliver a professional, quality service that exceeds the expectations of our valued clients.

Konnect Services adheres to the highest standards within the industry. All the necessary levels of insurance, health & safety and working-practice documentation are in place to enable us to work on all projects with any client. We offer total dedication and commitment to each and every project that we undertake.

Electrical

At Konnect Services, we are constantly expanding our services, integrating new and emerging technologies. Our electrical capability runs from mains and sub-mains distribution and critical power through to life-safety, security, lighting and data infrastructure.

Discuss a electrical project
Electrical services · 12
  1. E.01

    CCTV & Security Systems

    Design and installation of CCTV, intruder alarms and access control systems, including integrated surveillance and remote monitoring.

    We design and install robust security solutions including high-definition CCTV, intruder alarms, and access control systems. Our integrated systems offer 24/7 surveillance, remote monitoring, and high deterrent value to safeguard your people, assets, and property.

  2. E.02

    Light and Power Requirements

    Design, installation and commissioning of lighting and power systems for new facilities and existing buildings.

    We provide full design, installation, and commissioning of lighting and power systems tailored to your specific environment. Whether you’re constructing a new facility or upgrading an existing one, our solutions ensure safe, efficient, and reliable electrical distribution that meets regulatory standards and energy goals.

  3. E.03

    Emergency Lighting Systems

    Design, installation, testing and maintenance of emergency lighting systems supporting safe evacuation and regulatory requirements.

    Our emergency lighting systems are designed to activate automatically during power failures, providing safe evacuation routes in compliance with legal safety requirements. We offer everything from design and installation to testing and maintenance to ensure your facility remains safe at all times.

  4. E.04

    Intelligent Lighting

    Sensor-controlled and automated lighting systems designed to improve lighting performance, comfort and energy efficiency.

    Our intelligent lighting systems use sensors and automated controls to optimise lighting performance and energy consumption. Perfect for commercial buildings, warehouses, and offices, these solutions enhance comfort, reduce utility costs, and support sustainable building practices.

    • Commercial buildings
    • Warehouses
    • Offices
  5. E.05

    Critical Power Systems

    Backup power solutions, including UPS systems and generators, for environments where continuity of power is essential.

    We provide reliable backup power systems such as uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) and generators. These systems are essential for environments where power continuity is critical, including hospitals, data centres, and manufacturing plants.

    • Hospitals
    • Data centres
    • Manufacturing plants
  6. E.06

    Thermographic Electrical Surveys and Reports

    Infrared thermographic surveys to identify hotspots and potential electrical faults before they develop into more serious problems.

    Using infrared thermographic imaging, we perform detailed surveys to detect hotspots and anomalies in electrical systems before they lead to failures or hazards. Our reports help you plan preventative maintenance, reduce downtime, and extend the lifespan of your electrical infrastructure.

  7. E.07

    Mains and Sub-mains Distribution

    Design and installation of main and sub-main distribution boards for safe and scalable electrical distribution.

    We specialise in the design and installation of main and sub-main distribution boards, ensuring safe and balanced power delivery across all areas of your building. Our installations are scalable and built to handle future expansion and increased power demands.

  8. E.08

    Fire Alarm Systems

    Supply, installation and maintenance of fire detection and alarm systems.

    We supply, install, and maintain advanced fire alarm systems that offer early warning and rapid response capabilities. Whether for commercial, industrial, or residential properties, our fire detection solutions are compliant with current fire safety regulations and tailored to your specific risk profile.

    • Commercial
    • Industrial
    • Residential
  9. E.09

    Voice Alarms

    Voice alarm systems providing clear emergency instructions and communication for building occupants.

    Our voice alarm systems ensure clear and effective communication during emergencies, guiding occupants to safety with intelligible pre-recorded or live messages. These systems are essential for large public venues, schools, commercial buildings, and healthcare facilities.

    • Public venues
    • Schools
    • Commercial buildings
    • Healthcare
  10. E.10

    Maintenance and Repair Services

    Planned and reactive electrical maintenance and repair.

    We provide scheduled and reactive maintenance for all electrical systems to ensure ongoing performance and compliance. Our responsive repair team addresses issues quickly, minimising downtime and maintaining safety across your operations.

  11. E.11

    Data and IT Installations

    Data cabling, network design, installation and IT infrastructure solutions.

    We offer complete data cabling and IT infrastructure solutions, including network design, installation, and configuration. Our services support high-speed connectivity, robust communication systems, and future-ready IT growth.

  12. E.12

    Certification, Technical Inspection and Testing Services

    Electrical inspection, testing and certification to confirm compliance with applicable standards.

    We carry out thorough inspections and testing of all electrical installations, issuing certification that confirms compliance with national safety and performance standards. Ideal for landlords, business owners, and facility managers seeking peace of mind and legal assurance.

    • Landlords
    • Business owners
    • Facility managers

Mechanical

Water, air, heating, cooling and fire protection — the mechanical systems that keep buildings comfortable, efficient and safe. We install, test, balance and commission mechanical systems to operate to their design specification, then support them with planned maintenance and rapid-response repairs.

Discuss a mechanical project
Mechanical services · 10
  1. M.01

    Hot and Cold Water Services

    Installation and maintenance of hot and cold water systems, including booster sets, water heaters and storage tanks.

    We install and maintain efficient hot and cold water systems for residential, commercial, and industrial applications. From booster sets to water heaters and storage tanks, we ensure consistent pressure and temperature with every installation.

    • Residential
    • Commercial
    • Industrial
  2. M.02

    Refrigeration and Air Conditioning

    Supply, installation and maintenance of HVAC systems, office air conditioning and industrial chillers.

    We supply, install, and maintain HVAC systems that regulate indoor temperature and humidity. From office air conditioning to industrial chillers, our systems improve energy efficiency while maintaining optimal working environments.

  3. M.03

    Process and Chilled Water Systems

    Installation of process cooling and chilled water distribution systems for industrial environments.

    We install tailored systems for process cooling and chilled water distribution, supporting industrial operations that require precise temperature control. Our solutions are built for reliability, efficiency, and low maintenance.

  4. M.04

    Drainage Pipe Work

    Installation, repair and maintenance of soil, waste and stormwater drainage systems.

    We provide complete drainage solutions, from installation to repair and maintenance of soil, waste, and stormwater systems. Our designs prevent blockages and water damage, supporting the long-term integrity of your building.

  5. M.05

    Mechanical Ventilation

    Ventilation systems designed to supply fresh air, extract stale air and help control indoor air quality.

    Our mechanical ventilation systems ensure a constant supply of fresh air while extracting stale air, helping to control humidity, odours, and air quality. Ideal for office buildings, schools, and industrial spaces, our systems promote health and comfort.

    • Office buildings
    • Schools
    • Industrial spaces
  6. M.06

    LPG Systems

    Design and installation of LPG systems for heating, cooking and industrial applications.

    We design and install safe and compliant LPG systems for heating, cooking, and industrial use. Our engineers are fully qualified and ensure that your system is efficient, leak-free, and meets all safety and performance regulations.

  7. M.07

    Thermal Insulation

    Insulation for pipework, ductwork and mechanical equipment to improve efficiency and reduce heat loss.

    We supply and install thermal insulation for pipework, ductwork, and mechanical equipment to improve energy efficiency and reduce heat loss. This service is essential for maintaining comfort and reducing operational costs.

  8. M.08

    Fire Protection Systems

    Fire protection installations including sprinklers, gas suppression and specialist extinguishing systems.

    We design and install comprehensive fire protection systems including sprinklers, gas suppression, and specialised extinguishing equipment. Our solutions are tailored to your building’s layout and use, ensuring optimal protection and compliance with fire codes.

  9. M.09

    Balancing and Commissioning

    Testing, balancing and commissioning of mechanical systems to ensure they operate according to their design specifications.

    We ensure mechanical systems such as HVAC and plumbing are fully tested, balanced, and commissioned to operate at peak performance. This service verifies that systems meet design specifications and operate efficiently from day one.

  10. M.10

    Maintenance and Repair Services

    Planned mechanical maintenance and reactive repair services.

    We provide ongoing mechanical system support through planned maintenance and rapid-response repairs. Our goal is to reduce system downtime, improve efficiency, and ensure long-term reliability.

R / 03 — Discipline4 services

Renewable Energy

We are accredited installers of renewable energy systems. From solar PV and solar thermal to air and ground source heat pumps, we help buildings generate cleaner energy, lower running costs and reduce carbon emissions.

Discuss a renewable energy project
Renewable Energy services · 04
  1. R.01

    Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Systems

    Commercial and industrial solar PV installations designed to generate renewable electricity and reduce reliance on conventional energy sources.

    We supply and install high-performance solar PV systems for commercial and industrial use. Our systems help lower electricity bills and carbon emissions while offering excellent return on investment through renewable energy generation.

    • Commercial
    • Industrial
  2. R.02

    Solar Thermal Systems

    Solar water heating systems for domestic and commercial hot water applications.

    We offer solar water heating systems that capture and use solar energy to supply domestic or commercial hot water needs. This eco-friendly solution reduces energy costs and carbon footprint while ensuring a steady hot water supply.

    • Domestic
    • Commercial
  3. R.03

    Air Source Heat Pumps

    Installation of air source heat pumps for energy-efficient building heating and hot water.

    We install energy-efficient air source heat pumps that extract heat from the outside air — even in cold conditions — to heat your building and water. These systems reduce energy bills and are a great alternative to traditional boilers.

  4. R.04

    Ground Source Heat Pumps

    Ground source heat pump installations using underground temperatures to provide heating and cooling.

    We offer ground source heat pump installations that utilise underground temperatures to provide consistent and sustainable heating and cooling. These systems are highly efficient, long-lasting, and ideal for properties with space for ground loops or boreholes.
